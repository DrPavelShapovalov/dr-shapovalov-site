How bone grows

If the lower jaw is underdeveloped, it can be lengthened. The bone is cut, a small device is attached, and the fragments are moved apart little by little. New bone of the child’s own grows in the gap. Scroll down and the device will start working.

0,0мм
  1. Surgery
  2. Pause
  3. Lengthening
  4. Rest
Lengthening

The device is turned a little each day, usually about a millimetre per day. The fragments move apart and new bone fills the gap.

This is a diagram, not a patient’s scan. The amount and rate of lengthening are planned for each child individually.

Образование, курсы и то, где я работаю сейчас.

Since 2021 I have worked as an oral and maxillofacial surgeon in the Department of Surgical Treatment of Craniomaxillofacial Anomalies, Pediatric Clinic of Maxillofacial Surgery, Central Research Institute of Dental and Maxillofacial Surgery (Moscow). I operate on children and teenagers: I place mandibular distraction devices, expand the upper and lower jaws, remove jaw cysts and benign tumours, and perform bone grafting. On my blog I show real cases with numbers and terms that make sense to both patients and colleagues.

Odontoma8 years oldbefore and 1 year later

Mandibular odontoma in an 8-year-old

  • Referred from Tambov with the diagnosis “Benign neoplasm of the left mandibular body. Odontoma? Cementoma?”
  • : multiple irregular masses within the left mandibular body. The buccal, lingual and inferior cortical plates were intact; the bone bulged on the vestibular side
  • Surgery under combined endotracheal anaesthesia: the buccal cortical plate was raised as a lid with a piezosurgical approach, all masses — about 50 — were removed, the cavity was irrigated with dimethyl sulfoxide solution and closed primarily
  • Primary teeth over the lesion were extracted for access. The follow-up showed the permanent tooth germs undamaged and the cortical plate preserved along its whole length
  • Histology: odontoma confirmed

Odontogenic tumour15 years oldbefore and 9 months later

Odontogenic ghost cell tumour: the jaw kept without resection

  • A lesion in the body and ramus of the left mandible was found by chance. The May 2025 biopsy was inconclusive: odontogenic fibroma and fibrous osteodysplasia
  • was proposed: remove a segment of the mandible and fix a load-bearing titanium plate
  • Surgery on 10 Nov 2025 — : the pathological tissue was removed from inside the bone, the lower border and continuity of the mandible were preserved. Teeth 37 and 38 within the lesion were extracted
  • Histology: odontogenic ghost cell tumour, benign (ICD-O 9302/0)
  • 9 months later, in August 2026: the swelling of the left mandible is gone
